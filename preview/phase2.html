Phase 2

Automatic panel layout on a roof drawn in AutoCAD

Start from the roof as the architect drew it: outline, parapet and everything standing on it. The code keeps panels clear of each obstacle and its winter shadow, packs the rest, and hands back CAD plus a 3D model to walk around in VR or AR.

Open the 3D roof (Orbit, VR, AR) Customer roof viewer (sun, shadows, AR) Download the model (.glb)

Key numbers

12 x 8 mroof, L-shaped
12panels placed
6.6 kWat 550 W per panel
4obstacles: stair, store, 2 tanks
15°panel tilt
19° Nlatitude for winter shadows

The same 12 x 8 m roof with nothing on it takes 27 panels (about 14.9 kW): the obstacles and their shadows cost more than half the space. The measurements are placeholders until a real drawing comes in.

How it works

  1. Read the roof from the AutoCAD drawing: outline (with the L-shaped notch), slab, parapet height, and each structure on it.
  2. Grow every obstacle by its footprint plus the shadow it throws at winter noon (sun altitude from the latitude), and keep a setback from the parapet.
  3. Fill what is left with tilted panels in rows spaced so they do not shade each other.
  4. Export a STEP solid for AutoCAD, a GLB for the 3D viewer, and a JSON sidecar the viewer reads for the roof details.
