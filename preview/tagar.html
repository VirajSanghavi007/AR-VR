How it works

  1. Print 4 AprilTags (36h11, ids 0 to 3 by default, from scripts/make_tags.py) and lay them flat at the 4 corners of a rectangle on a table or the floor.
  2. Press Start AR, move the phone until the ring sits on the surface, then aim at the middle of the 4 tags so all of them are in view.
  3. The scanned terrace design snaps into the rectangle and scales itself to fit. It is pinned to the table: walk around it, it stays put.
  4. Fix the scale with − / + (steps to round ratios like 1:20, 1:25), Fit to go back, Rotate to turn it 90°.

Tag size is the black square's edge. It is only a check: the app measures each tag in AR and warns if the surface was misjudged.
