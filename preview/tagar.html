Tabletop AR on AprilTags

A quick table demo: panels (or a SketchUp model) on a few tags. For full size on site, use the Site Designer. Also: check your printed tags · customer viewer.

How it works

  1. Print AprilTags (36h11, from scripts/make_tags.py), any ids, each tag laid any way up. The page counts them itself: 2 tags at opposite corners of a rectangle, or side by side at the two ends of one edge (the panels then fill the square behind them); 3 tags at 3 corners; 4 tags at all 4 corners.
  2. Press Start AR, move the phone until the ring sits on the surface, then aim between the tags so all of them are in view.
  3. A solar panel snaps into the rectangle, scaled to fill it (− and + pick a round 1:N scale). It is pinned to the table: walk around it, it stays put. Move the tags and it follows them.
  4. Type how many rows and columns of panels you want (below, or in AR at the bottom). The array is laid out again at once; while the scale is on auto fit, it is resized to fill the tags.
  5. Tap a panel to pick it; long-press and drag to move it; twist two fingers to turn the picked panel, or the whole array when none is picked. Look changes the panel type, − / + / Fit set the scale.
  6. The top bar shows the array's peak power and a rough energy estimate: kWh per day and the average watts (Wh per hour) over the day, from the peak sun hours below and ~20% losses.
  7. Photo saves what the phone sees with the panels in it, captioned with the panel type, count, power and energy, to share with the client.

Panel type

Testing: a sample model (solar pergola) is already loaded below, and a sample room (scanned office, tags on its back wall 1.20 m from the left corner, 1.10 m up). Pick your own files to replace it, or open the page with ?samples=0 to start empty.

Tag size is the black square's edge. It is only a check: the app measures each tag in AR and warns if the surface was misjudged.

Designer's model (optional)

A .glb from the design team, ideally in metres, or a SketchUp export: in SketchUp, File > Export > 3D Model, COLLADA (.dae) or OBJ (pick the .obj together with its .mtl and texture files). .fbx, .dwg or .rvt: convert to .glb first, e.g. in Blender. Once the tags lock, press Place model and tap the wall or floor where it goes; Turn and Scale adjust it, 1 m bar lays a 1 m striped bar (10 cm stripes) beside it to check against a tape measure, Save pose downloads where it is.

Room model (Phase 3)

A .glb (or SketchUp .dae / .obj) of the whole room (walls, floor, ceiling) to line up with the real one and walk around inside. Tape the tags on one wall, pick which wall of the model that is, and type where the tags' centre is: its distance from that wall's left corner (facing the wall) and its height above the floor. In AR, turn round: the model's corners and edges (cyan lines) should sit on the real ones; Right and Up nudge it, See-through swaps solid and ghost walls.
