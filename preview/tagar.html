How it works

  1. Print AprilTags (36h11, from scripts/make_tags.py). 2 tags (ids 0 and 1): lay them at opposite corners of a rectangle, both the same way up. 4 tags (ids 0-3): one at each corner.
  2. Press Start AR, move the phone until the ring sits on the surface, then aim between the tags so all of them are in view.
  3. The scanned roof snaps into the rectangle, scaled to fit, with one solar panel on it. It is pinned to the table: walk around it, it stays put. Move the tags and it follows them.
  4. Long-press the panel and drag to move it; with it picked up, put a second finger down and twist to turn it. Look changes the panel type, − / + / Fit set the scale.

Panel type

Tag size is the black square's edge. It is only a check: the app measures each tag in AR and warns if the surface was misjudged.
