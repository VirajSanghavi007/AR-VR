Site designer (Phase 1)

Sketch a design on the real site in AR. Printed AprilTags round the site keep it locked in place from every side, a customer walks round it, flips between options A, B and C, and you save it to reopen or share. Other pages: tabletop AR, design viewer, tag check.

  1. Print 36h11 tags (scripts/make_tags.py), tape 2 or more on each wall about 1.2-1.5 m up, at least 2 m apart round the site. Each id once.
  2. Start AR, aim at the first wall's tags and press Lock. Keep Map on and walk round once: every new tag is added to the map.
  3. Aim at the floor so the floor is found, then draw: tap corners for an outline or a wall line, or place blocks from the library.
  4. Show: before/after, options A/B/C, size labels, photo and video. Save the design to reopen it locked to the same tags.
